## Lesson

The WSL wall clock steps back ~1 s every ~30 s here: any test with a 1 s
margin between a sleep and a wall-clock threshold flakes (early-exit
'after 4 s' failed 9/120). Widen margins or use monotonic time
(BACKLOG § From Nocturne D9 P1).
