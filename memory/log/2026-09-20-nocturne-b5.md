## Lessons

- **A kill is only a kill when the process is gone.** No test asserted the
  OS process died after `DELETE`; a fake CLI trapping HUP+TERM would have
  caught it years ago. Now `tests/sessions-kill.test.ts` does.
- **Signal the group, then check the group.** Leader-only signals miss
  wrappers; leader-exit-only guards call the ladder off too early.
- **Two vacuous tests caught by re-planting** (T10, T12): a sleep-and-hope
  wait passed with the mutant in place. Wait on the observable line, never
  on time.
- **Session resume after a limit kill**: the transcript (`~/.claude/projects/
  …/<session>.jsonl`) names every agent spawn and the last brief; the tree
  plus the suite tell the rest. Ten minutes to know exactly where it died.
